# Restaurant direction

Gina wants Peploe's to feel warm, confident and contemporary while preserving
recognition for loyal guests. The site should welcome more diners aged 35–55
without making everyday lunch and dinner feel reserved for special occasions.
She rejects fine-dining positioning, corporate styling, excessive gold, marble
and script-font luxury cues.

Photography should show service, people, food arriving and the family. Empty
rooms and isolated dishes alone do not express the intended hospitality.
The descent from St Stephen's Green is an optional creative direction, not an
animation requirement. The artist behind the restaurant's name should not
dominate the family story.

A People & Produce section is a possible future addition, not a launch promise.
Use real supplier relationships only after owner approval. Weddings should feel
like intimate city celebrations, not generic packages.

An unavailable online date does not prove the restaurant is full: the team
manages some dates and tables directly. Preserve that distinction in guest copy.

The owner approved temporary reuse of the Growers article body where the design
supplies cards but no article copy. That approval does not make those articles
final editorial content. Restaurant approval of legal copy, hours, capacities,
prices and public editorial content remains outstanding.
